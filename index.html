01 / THE STORY

From the factory floorto the decision room.

I began on the production line, where a wasted hour is something you can see and count. That habit, asking what is actually happening, followed me into analytics, supply chain, strategy and now central planning at Midas Safety.

My work starts with a practical question: what decision are we trying to make?

Then I look at what data, systems, analytics and now AI can do to answer it. The tools changed with every role. That question never did.
