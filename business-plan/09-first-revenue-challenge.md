# Part 10 — First Revenue Challenge: "First paying customer in 30 days"

**Rule:** VALIDATE → SELL → DELIVER → IMPROVE → SCALE. Nothing gets polished past "good enough to show a buyer". Budget for the 30 days: **≤ PKR 60,000**.

**Win condition:** money received in Payoneer/bank from a client outside your personal network by Day 30.
**Fallback win:** a signed proposal + deposit, *or* ≥ 5 discovery calls held (proves demand; close in Days 31–45).

Daily time: Mon–Fri ~1.5 h, Sat ~4 h, Sun ~1 h.
