## What competitors could do

- **Generalist BI freelancers relabel themselves "supply chain"** — they can copy words, not the PHI benchmark, ERP mapping kits or your ability to run an S&OP conversation. Keep publishing specific planning insight to make the gap visible.
- **Planning SaaS vendors lower prices / add Power BI connectors** — position as vendor-neutral; offer to configure their tool where clients already have one (partner, don't fight).
- **ERP partners build in-house manufacturing BI** — turn them into white-label partners first.
- **Indian planning consultancies** offer similar services cheaper — compete on specialism, speed and fixed price, not hourly rate.

## Why customers might refuse to pay

1. "We'll do it ourselves with Copilot" → Show the *planning logic* (RCCP, safety stock) — not the visuals.
2. "Budget freeze / tariffs / downturn" → Lead with cash release from excess inventory (self-funding).
3. "Data security / offshore" → Work in their tenant, NDA, DPA, no data on personal devices, offer a security one-pager.
4. "Not a priority" → Trigger-based targeting only (hiring a planner, ERP change, OTIF penalties).
5. "Too small/unknown provider" → Money-back guarantee, fixed price, small first step, visible case studies.

## What would make you quit

- 6 weeks of outreach with no replies → solved by diagnosing the funnel (see table below), not by willpower.
- First difficult client (scope creep, slow payment) → fixed-scope proposals, 50% upfront, change-request clause.
- EMBA exam periods → pre-plan 2 "maintenance weeks" each term at 50% quota.
- Comparing yourself to online "$10k/month" stories → measure against your own tracker only.

## Pakistan-specific operating notes **[V — verify with a tax adviser]**

- **Payments:** PayPal does not operate for Pakistan residents; Wise does not generally onboard Pakistan-resident individuals; use **Payoneer** (Upwork withdrawals and invoices) and a bank **USD/Freelancer Digital Account** for wires.
- **Tax:** register NTN on FBR IRIS and become a filer (Active Taxpayer List); register with **PSEB** as a freelancer/IT exporter — IT export remittances through banking channels have historically attracted a reduced final tax rate for PSEB-registered exporters (rates have changed across recent budgets — confirm the current FY rate). Keep all bank remittance certificates (PRCs).
- **Entity:** sole proprietorship is fine to start; consider SECP SMC-Private Limited after ~PKR 1M/month or when clients require a company.
- **Contracts:** governing law/venue clause, NDA, IP clause (client owns deliverables; you keep pre-existing tools).
