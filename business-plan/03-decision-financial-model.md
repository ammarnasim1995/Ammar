# Part 4 — The One Business to Start

## Decision

> **If I were in your position, I would start a productized "Planning & Plant Performance Control Tower" service for $10–150M manufacturers and industrial distributors in the US, UK and GCC — sold as a fixed-price 10-day Planning Health Diagnostic, converted into a Control Tower Build, and then into a monthly "Planning Analyst-as-a-Service" retainer — because it is the only option where your hardest-to-copy asset (real IBP, capacity-planning, SAP PP, OEE and KPI experience inside a global manufacturer) *is* the product, it earns in USD from Month 2–3, needs under PKR 150k of your PKR 500k, fits 10–15 hrs/week (all work is asynchronous except one call per client per month), and has a built-in path from one-off projects to recurring revenue to a productized micro-SaaS.**

Working brand used in this plan: **Takt Analytics** — *"IBP-grade planning visibility for factories that will never buy IBP."* (Name options and availability checks in Part 6.)

### Why not the others (one line each)

| Rejected | Reason |
|---|---|
| Fractional S&OP analyst (standalone) | Pure time-for-money, high trust barrier when cold → becomes the retainer tier instead |
| White-label for ERP partners | Lower margin, no client ownership → becomes a *channel* from Month 2 |
| DTC inventory analytics | Crowded founder inboxes, SaaS substitutes → becomes a *vertical page* in Month 4+ |
| AI order-entry automation | Highest AI/ERP-vendor commoditization risk, engineering-heavy → *add-on* only |
| GCC KPI office | 4–9-month sales cycles, relationship-driven → Year-2 upsell |

### How to read this

| Insight | Implication |
|---|---|
| **Peak capital used is only PKR 100–140k in every scenario.** | Do **not** deploy the full PKR 500k. Keep ~PKR 350k as a reserve. Capital is not your constraint — *hours and trust* are. |
| Conservative scenario reaches **PKR ~450k/month by M12** — just under your PKR 500k target. | The target is reachable only if you get **2+ retainers**. Recurring revenue is the single most important lever. |
| Base scenario crosses **PKR 500k/month in Month 5** and reaches **~PKR 1.34M/month** by M12. | Requires ~2 projects/month + 4 retainers by M12, a part-time contractor from M7, and ≥50% of revenue from direct channels. |
| Aggressive requires **11 paying clients** and **PKR 300k/month contractor** by M12. | At 15 hrs/week this is only feasible with strong SOPs and a templated data model (Part 5, Days 61–90). Treat it as the upside, not the plan. |
| Effective hourly rate check (Base, M6): PKR 588k revenue / ~60 hrs ≈ **USD 35/hr**; M12: PKR 1.34M / ~60 hrs of your time ≈ **USD 80/hr** (because the contractor does ~50% of delivery). | Leverage comes from contractors + templates + retainers, not from working more hours. |
